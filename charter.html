Project Charter

Scope, objectives, stakeholders and success criteria, agreed before any code was written.

1. Project Overview

FitTrack is a mobile application that lets users log workouts, track daily steps, and build habit streaks with a lightweight, gamified UI. This charter covers the build of a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to be released to a closed beta group of 500 users.

2. Business Case

User research (12 interviews + a 200-respondent survey) identified strong demand for a "low-friction" fitness tracker — most competitors were rated as too complex for casual users. FitTrack targets this gap: log a workout in under 10 seconds.

3. Objectives

  • Deliver a functional MVP (workout logging, step tracking, streaks) within 6 weeks.
  • Onboard 500 beta users within 2 weeks of launch.
  • Achieve Day-7 retention ≥ 30% in the beta cohort.
  • Keep MVP scope to 3 core features only — resist scope creep.

4. Scope

In scope (MVP): - Manual workout logging (type, duration, notes) - Daily step count (via phone sensor) - Streak tracking + push notification reminders

Out of scope (post-MVP / backlog): - Social sharing / friend feed - Integration with wearables (Apple Watch, Fitbit) - Nutrition tracking

7. Constraints & Assumptions

  • Fixed 5-person team, no additional hires during the project.
  • iOS only for MVP (Android post-MVP).
  • Budget covers 6 weeks of team time only — no paid user acquisition.
